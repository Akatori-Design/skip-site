How do I play?

Tap the moment the stone touches the water. A tap right as it lands skims it flat and fast; tapping in the air drops it short and high. Chain good skips to build speed.

I bought an upgrade but don't have it

Open Collection → Extras and tap Restore purchases. Upgrades are tied to your Apple ID.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account on both phones. Your stones, coins and records follow you.

How do I delete my account?

Open your account in the game and choose Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is removed.
