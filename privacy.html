Skip: Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Skip: Stone Skipping ("Skip") is a game. This policy explains what it stores, why, and how you can delete it. In short: you can play without an account, there are no ads, and nothing is used for tracking.

Without an account

If you never sign in, everything stays on your device: your best distances, unlocked stones and splashes, coins and settings. Nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Apple or Google to keep your progress on another device and to appear on the leaderboards. When you do, we store:

  • An account ID created by our sign-in service (Google Firebase Authentication), which also keeps the email address Apple or Google shares with it (with Apple you can choose to hide it). We do not receive your password, and the game itself does not use or show your email.
  • Your player name: your first name from Apple or Google, or the nickname you choose. It is shown on the leaderboards.
  • Your best distances (lake and Free style) and number of skips, shown on the leaderboards.
  • Your saved progress: unlocked items, coins, total distance and settings, so they can be restored.
  • An invite code, if you share one, and a record of which friend joined with it.

We never sell or share your information, or use it for advertising or tracking.

Purchases

Upgrades are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns which upgrades you own.

Where it is kept

Account data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in the United States. Firebase may keep standard technical logs needed to run its service.

Deleting your data

In the game, open your account and choose Delete account. This removes your leaderboard records, saved progress, invite code and the account itself. You can also contact us using the details below.

Children

Skip does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is described here, and an account is never required to play.
